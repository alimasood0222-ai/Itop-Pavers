# iTop Pavers – Brand Guidelines (for website design & development)

Source: "ITOP_APRESENTAÇÃO FINAL 1 4.pdf", the Brand Presentation / Visual Identity by Adapt Crew (adaptcrew.com), 2024, 58 pages. The deck is in both English and Portuguese.

## Brand essentials
- **Brand family:** iTop Group, with sub-brands **iTop Hardscape**, **iTop Builder** and **iTop Showroom**. Each sub-brand uses the same logo with its descriptor under the wordmark.
  - For the Pavers website, use **iTop Hardscape** (or iTop Group if the client asks for that).
- **Slogan:** "Crafting unique home solutions" (PT: "Criando soluções únicas para casas").
- **Secondary taglines used in the mockups:**
  - "Make your home as unique as you"
  - "Building legacies one project at a time"
  - "Blend creativity and functionality"
- **Voice and tone:** Trustworthy, solution-oriented, professional.
- **Positioning:** a solid, bold, trustworthy group with over 10 years of experience, focused on home solutions.

## Logo
- **Symbol:** a geometric house mark that combines:
  - the letters i, T, O and P
  - an upward arrow (growth)
  - a Formula 1 flag
  - a square house shape
  - buildings in perspective
  - a pen nib (closing the contract)
- **Wordmark:** "iTOP" in a custom blocky geometric face with angled cuts. The dot of the "i" is an orange square.
- **Lockup:** symbol on the left, wordmark on the right, descriptor (Group / Hardscape / Builder / Showroom) in a small spaced sans under or beside "P".
- **Approved colourways:**
  - On orange: white symbol, black wordmark, white descriptor.
  - On black: orange symbol, white wordmark, orange descriptor.
  - On white: orange symbol, black wordmark, orange descriptor.
- **Signature device:** a photo cropped inside the house-symbol silhouette. It's used for the logo variations, the badge photo, the email signature and banners.

## Typography
- **Title / display: Universo.** A wide, extended geometric sans used in bold for headings, names and taglines (for example "FABIO HESTEFERSON" on the cards and "Crafting unique home solutions" on the signage).
- **Body: Forma.** A sans family with weights from Thin through Black.
- **Presentation-only font:** the deck itself uses a condensed heavy display face for section titles ("BRAND PRESENTATION" and so on). It's a presentation style, not one of the specified brand fonts.
- **Web note:** confirm web-font licences for Universo and Forma, and get the font files from the client or Adapt Crew. If they aren't available, choose the nearest free substitutes and agree them with the client.

## Graphic language
- Strong orange/black colour blocking and diagonal bands at 45°, cut from the angled strokes of the logo.
- Angled "tab" labels (parallelograms) for names and URLs.
- Chevron/step dividers made of repeating angled segments.
- Thin line patterns in the style of a paver herringbone grid.
- Photos framed in angled or house-shaped crops.
- Dark backgrounds used heavily (black or near-black) with orange accents. White layouts appear on the letterhead and the hoarding (construction-fence) banner.
